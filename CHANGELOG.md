# Librarian

## 1.3.2

- The objective tracker section is now off by default, with a warning under its checkbox:
  Blizzard's tracker does not really allow addon sections, so it can cause Lua errors in combat
  and stop the tracker from updating. Type /reload after turning it off. Existing installs get
  it switched off once too, with a chat message; turn it back on in Options if you want it.

## 1.3.1

- The third reward goal (25 books) now needs level 35, up from 30, as in the current Forever
  data.
- Truthseeker's Bow now shows its level 40 requirement, like the other two rewards of that
  goal.

## 1.3.0

- New option: Show on continent maps (under World map pins, on by default). Turn it off to keep
  the pins on zone maps only.
- New option: Pin size, 50% to 200%, for the world map pins.

## 1.2.2

- Ataeric: On Arcane Curiosities is back: players now find it in The Sepulcher (Silverpine
  Forest), downstairs in the main building beside Sebastian Meloche.
- Updated note for A Luddite's Guide to Caring for Your Demonic Pet: where the Rusted Cage Key
  drops.

## 1.2.1

- The minimap button now goes through LibDBIcon, so minimap button bags (Leatrix Plus and
  others) pick it up. It keeps its position.
- Book data refreshed: flavour text for 11 more books, new player confirmations for
  Undead Potatoes, Scourge (Plaguewood), The Knight and the Lady (second spot), Demons and You
  and Legends of the Tidesages, and updated zone level ranges.

## 1.2.0

- New reward: the third Greater Friend of the Library quest (25 books, level 30) with
  Truthseeker's Bow, Crest of Elucidation and Researcher's Night Light. The goal bar now runs
  to 25.
- Rewards page: shows the level each quest and item needs (red while you are below it), and
  scrolls.
- Field Researcher's Loop is no longer rogue-only.
- Books found in more than one place now list every spot: The Knight and the Lady, Scourge and
  A Study of the Light (Rumi already did). You can still loot each book once. A book with two
  spots in one zone shows once in the toast and the tracker.
- Updated locations and notes from the latest beta reports.

## 1.1.1

- Book order: your own faction's zones that you have the level for now come first; the rest
  follow by zone level as before. The order updates when you level up.

## 1.1.0

- New languages: German, French, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Simplified and
  Traditional Chinese. The whole addon is translated, including the location notes; container
  names and book descriptions use Blizzard's own text.

## 1.0.0

First release for WoW Forever.

- Journal: goal progress, summary cards, every book grouped by state and zone level.
- Book page with an embedded zone map, the zone's other books, manual marks and waypoints.
- Rewards page: every choice, filtered by class and talent spec, with your pick and your
  librarian's location.
- World map pins, zone toast, objective tracker section, TomTom support.
- English and Spanish.
